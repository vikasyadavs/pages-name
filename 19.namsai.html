Work History of Namsai

Namsai has long been known for agriculture, trade routes, and cultural craftsmanship. Over time, the district has grown into an emerging employment hub in Arunachal Pradesh.

Traditional Agriculture

Rice cultivation, tea farming, and horticulture have historically supported livelihoods across the district.

Artisans & Crafts

Local communities contributed through bamboo crafts, weaving, and handmade products.

Trade & Connectivity

Regional trade with nearby towns encouraged transport and small business development.

Job History

1980s–1990s

Government jobs and agriculture dominated employment opportunities.

2000s

Infrastructure and education sectors began expanding rapidly.

2010s–Present

Private businesses, healthcare, retail, and digital opportunities are growing steadily.

Current Job Market
