Work History of Tinsukia

From Industrial Heritage to Modern Prosperity

1960s
Oil Discovery & Industrial Growth - Tinsukia emerged as a major oil production hub with the establishment of the Assam Oil Company, transforming the region's economy.
1980s
Manufacturing Expansion - Development of petrochemical industries and manufacturing sectors, creating thousands of skilled jobs and fostering industrial development.
2000s
Modernization Era - Infrastructure development, tech adoption, and service sector growth transformed Tinsukia into a diversified employment hub.
2024
Digital Revolution - Today, Tinsukia stands as a dynamic region with opportunities in tech, healthcare, education, and sustainable industries.
