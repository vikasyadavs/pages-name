Employment Evolution

Journey from traditional to modern career opportunities

Pre-1950
Agriculture and traditional crafts dominated the economy. Agrarian society with family-based occupations and feudal structures.
1950-1980
Post-independence government expansion created administrative jobs, schools, hospitals, and public sector opportunities.
1980-2000
Private sector emergence with manufacturing, retail chains, and processing industries creating diverse employment sectors.
2000-2015
Technology and service sector growth brought IT services, healthcare expansion, logistics boom, and tourism development.
2015-Present
Digital transformation, startups, skill development, e-commerce, and sustainable business practices creating modern career paths.
