Work History of Annamayya

Annamayya district has historically relied on agriculture, artisan industries, and regional trade. Groundnut farming, silk weaving, and local crafts have shaped the economic culture of the district over generations.

Agriculture

Groundnut cultivation and horticulture have played a major role in rural employment and economic growth.

Artisans

Traditional handicrafts and weaving communities contributed significantly to local trade networks.

Trade Routes

The district has historically connected nearby regions through agricultural and market-based trade.
