Heritage & Economy

The Working Story of Anakapalli

From ancient trade routes to modern industrial corridors — Anakapalli's economic identity is layered, resilient, and deeply rooted in the land and its people.

Pre-Colonial Era

Fertile Lands & Artisan Guilds

Situated in the fertile coastal plains of the Godavari-Krishna delta region, Anakapalli's earliest economy was anchored in wet-land paddy cultivation. Artisan communities—weavers, potters, and bronze craftspeople—formed the backbone of local trade, supplying goods along ancient coastal routes to Visakhapatnam and beyond.

Colonial Period (1800s–1947)

Cash Crops, Cashew & the Rail Connection

Under British administration, the region transitioned toward commercial agriculture. Cashew cultivation became a defining industry for the hilly interiors surrounding Anakapalli. The arrival of the East Coast Railway line made Anakapalli a minor but critical junction for moving agricultural produce—paddy, groundnuts, and sugarcane—to port at Vizag. Cottage industries in handloom weaving also flourished during this era.

Post-Independence (1950s–1980s)

Cooperative Farming & Public Sector Growth

With land reforms and cooperative movements after independence, Anakapalli's farming communities reorganised. The broader Visakhapatnam industrial belt began drawing skilled and semi-skilled labour from the district. Government employment through schools, hospitals, and local administration grew substantially, forming a new middle class.

Liberalisation Era (1990s–2010s)

Pharmaceuticals, Cashew Processing & Urban Growth

Economic liberalisation brought new industries. Pharmaceutical manufacturers and agro-processing units began setting up along national highway corridors near Anakapalli. Cashew processing factories expanded significantly, providing employment—especially to women—in large numbers. Private engineering colleges and ITIs expanded the educated workforce available to industries in the region.

Employment Evolution

How Work Changed in Anakapalli

Each decade reshaped who worked, in what sectors, and under what conditions — from paddy fields to pharma labs.

1960s–70s

Agriculture Dominant

Over 70% of the workforce was engaged in farming — paddy, sugarcane, and cashew. Government employment was the aspirational peak, and industrial jobs were rare outside Vizag city limits.

↑ Farming ↑ Govt Jobs ↓ Industry
1980s–90s

Education & Services Rise

Expansion of schools and colleges created teaching and administrative roles. The opening of cooperative banks and government schemes drew youth into white-collar government jobs. Cashew factories employed women in large numbers for the first time.

↑ Education ↑ Women's Work ↓ Subsistence Farming
2000s

Pharma & Industrial Corridor

Anakapalli gained proximity to Visakhapatnam's booming pharma and chemical industries. Highway development attracted warehousing and logistics firms. Private engineering colleges produced a new generation of diploma and degree holders seeking factory and IT roles.

↑ Pharma ↑ Logistics ↑ Private Sector
2010s–Present

District Status & New Opportunities

Anakapalli was carved into a separate district in 2022, bringing a surge in administrative posts, new infrastructure investment, and heightened investor interest. IT services, healthcare, renewable energy, and organised retail are now the fastest-growing sectors.

↑ IT/BPO ↑ Healthcare ↑ Renewable Energy ↓ Manual Farm Labour
